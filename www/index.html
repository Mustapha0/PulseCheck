    Force a new tower

    Android apps cannot pick a tower. What works is making the network choose again, which can land you on a stronger one.

    1. Save the tower you are on now.
    2. Switch airplane mode on for 15 seconds, then off. Or reselect your carrier.
    3. When the bars return, compare.

    Real ping and route

    ICMP pings to 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8, plus the first hops of the route. Takes about 20 seconds. With Shizuku connected, the Test tab adds this too.

    Picking a specific tower or locking a band needs root. Shizuku cannot do that.
